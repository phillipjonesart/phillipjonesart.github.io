Color Chase

A p5.js game by Phillip Jones

Play

Color Chase is a game where the player moves their cursor around trying to collect as many dots as possible. The behavior that comes from this is a chain of dots that you drag around the screen with your cursor. At the very end, when you've maxed out what you can collect, there is a big visual and audio swell. The game is supposed to feel simple, but catchy and captivating. The game is really about capturing attention. I think everybody should play it because the payoff when you beat it is pretty satisfying.
